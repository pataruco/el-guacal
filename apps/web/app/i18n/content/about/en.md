title: About El Guacal
---

El Guacal helps you find Venezuelan products wherever you are. Whether you're looking for Harina P.A.N., Maltín Polar, or any other taste of home, the community keeps the map up to date with the shops that stock them.

## How it works

The data is collected and maintained by the community. Anyone can add a new store or update existing information to keep the map accurate and useful.

### After you add a store

A moderator checks every suggestion before it appears on the map. This keeps spam and bad data out. You can track your suggestions in **My store proposals** — they start as "Pending review" and you'll see when they're approved or rejected. Reviews usually take up to 72 hours. If we reject a suggestion, we leave a note explaining why.

## Open data

All store and product data is available as a free download in CSV and JSON formats. Visit the [Dataset](/en/dataset) page to get the latest export.
